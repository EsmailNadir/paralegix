# Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is the first thing you see when you sign in: what needs your attention today, drawn from across matters, tasks, billing, and messages. Open it from **Dashboard** at the top of the left sidebar.

Unlike the rest of the app, the dashboard is yours to arrange. The widgets, their order, and which stat cards appear are all per-person preferences, so two people at the same firm can have completely different dashboards.

## Widgets

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Welcome Banner** | A greeting and your firm |
| **Stat Cards** | A row of headline numbers (see below) |
| **Recent Matters** | Matters you have touched most recently |
| **Upcoming Tasks** | Tasks due soon, grouped by day |
| **My Assignments** | Tasks assigned to you |
| **Messages** | Recent conversations and unread replies |
| **Your Team** | The people at your firm |
| **Pending Signatures** | Signing requests still waiting on someone |
| **Financial Snapshot** | Billing headline figures |
| **Upcoming Payments** | Invoices coming due |
| **Upcoming Events** | Court dates and meetings ahead |
| **Calendar** | A compact month calendar |
| **Overdue Tasks** | Anything past its due date |
| **My Time** | Hours you have logged |

Some widgets are off by default. Turn any of them on from edit mode.

## Stat Cards

The stat card row is configurable too. Pick the four or so numbers you actually watch from:

Open Matters, Total Matters, Closed Matters, Pending Tasks, Completed Tasks, Total Tasks, Upcoming Deadlines, Overdue Tasks, Unread Messages, Conversations, Team Members, Outstanding A/R, Overdue Invoices, Collected This Month, Unbilled WIP, Hours Today, and Hours This Week.

A new dashboard starts with Open Matters, Total Matters, Pending Tasks, and Upcoming Deadlines.

## Rearranging It

Enter **edit mode** and each widget grows a small toolbar:

- **Drag** the handle to reorder widgets.
- **Move to sidebar** puts a widget in the narrow right-hand column, or brings it back to the main area.
- **Hide** takes a widget off your dashboard without losing its position.
- **Configure cards**, on the stat row, chooses which numbers appear.

Changes save as you make them, and they follow you to any device you sign in on.

::: tip
If a widget shows nothing useful for your role, hide it. A paralegal who never touches invoices has no reason to keep Financial Snapshot on screen.
:::

## What You Can See

Every widget respects your [role's permissions](/settings/roles). Someone without billing access does not see billing figures, even if the widget is switched on, and the numbers behind each card are scoped the same way. The dashboard never becomes a way around a permission.

## Customizing the Sidebar

Unlike the dashboard widgets above, sidebar visibility is a firm-wide setting, not a per-person one. A firm administrator can hide sections nobody at the firm uses, under **Settings > Customization**, so a firm that does not run trust accounts is not carrying that menu item for anyone.
