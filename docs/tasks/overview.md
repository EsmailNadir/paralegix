# Tasks

Tasks are the to-do items and deadlines your firm works through every day. A task can stand on its own or be tied to a matter, and each one can have an assignee, a due date, a priority, and a status. Open **Tasks** from the left sidebar to manage them.

## The Tasks List

The list view shows every task in your firm, one per row.

Each task shows its title, who it is assigned to, a status badge, a priority badge, and a due date. Tasks that are past due are highlighted so nothing slips through.

You can narrow the list down with the controls at the top:

- **Search** by task name, description, or client.
- **Filter** by assignee, matter, status, or priority.
- **Sort** by due date, title, status, priority, assignee, or matter.

### Statuses and Priorities

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Not started yet |
| **In Progress** | Being worked on |
| **Blocked** | Waiting on something else |
| **Completed** | Done |

Priorities run from **Low** to **Medium**, **High**, and **Urgent**, so the most important work is easy to spot.

## Creating a Task

Click **Create New Task** in the top right. You can set:

- A **title** and an optional **description**
- The **matter** it belongs to (optional)
- One or more **assignees**
- A **due date** and a **priority**

::: tip
Assigning a task to a matter keeps it in one place with the rest of that matter's work. You will also see the task on the matter's own **Tasks** tab.
:::

## Subtasks

Open any task to see its detail page, where you can break the work down into **subtasks**: a simple checklist under the task. Add as many as you need, check them off as you go, rename or reorder them, and watch the progress bar fill in. Subtasks are a lightweight way to track the steps within a larger task without creating separate tasks for each one.

## Updating and Completing Tasks

- Click the **edit** icon on any task to change its details.
- Use the quick controls on each row to update status or timing.
- Mark a task **Completed** when it is done. Completed tasks stay on record and can still be found through search and filters.

::: info
Tasks with a due date can appear on your calendar and can trigger reminders, so deadlines stay visible without extra work.
:::

## Notes

Every task has its own **Notes** tab, separate from subtasks, for the discussion and updates around the work rather than the checklist itself. Add a note, and anyone on the task can reply underneath it, so a question and its answer stay attached to the note they belong to instead of scattering across the task. Pin a note to keep it at the top, and open a note's history to see every edit that has been made to it.

## Logging Billable Time from a Task

Work on a task is billable, so you can capture time without leaving the task list. Each task row has a **timer** (play icon) and a **Log time** (clock icon) shortcut. Both create a time entry that is pre-filled from the task and linked back to it.

A task does not have its own invoice. The time you log rolls up into the task's matter (or client), where it can be added to that matter's invoice. See [Billing](/billing/overview) for logging time and [Invoices](/billing/invoices) for how it becomes an invoice.
