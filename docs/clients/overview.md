# Clients

Clients are the people and organizations your firm represents. Each client has a profile that ties together their matters, tasks, documents, messages, and billing. Open **Clients** from the left sidebar to manage them.

::: tip
Not signed on yet? Prospective clients live on the **Leads** tab of this same page. See [Leads](/leads/overview).
:::

## The Clients List

The list shows every client in your firm, with the essentials on each row.

For each client you can see their name, status, email, phone number, and a quick count of how many matters and tasks they are attached to. Use the **search** box at the top to find a client by name or email.

The list is paged and both the search and the paging run on the server, so a firm with thousands of clients opens just as quickly as one with a dozen.

## Adding a Client

Click **Add Client** in the top right and fill in what you know:

- **Name** (a person or an organization)
- **Email** and **phone**
- Optional **organization**, **address**, and preferred contact method

You can add a client on their own, or attach them to a matter as you create it.

::: tip
The email you enter is also used if you later invite the client to the **client portal**, where they can view their matters, documents, and invoices.
:::

## The Client Profile

Click a client to open their profile page.

The header shows the client's contact details and status. Below it, tabs organize everything connected to that client:

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Profile details and a billing snapshot |
| **Matters** | The matters this client is involved in |
| **Tasks** | Work tied to the client's matters |
| **Events** | Meetings and calendar items |
| **Notes** | Internal notes about the client. Works the same as [matter notes](/matters/overview#notes): reply threads, pinning, and edit history |
| **Contacts** | Additional people at the client, drawn from the firm [contact directory](/contacts/overview) |
| **Messages** | Conversations with this client. See [Messages](/messaging/overview) |
| **Time Tracking** | Billable time recorded for the client |
| **Billing** | The client's invoices and balance |
| **Files** | Documents shared with or about the client. See [Files](/files/overview) |
| **Document Requests** | Checklists you have posted to this client, both matter-specific and general. See [Documents and Calendar](/automation/documents-and-calendar#document-requests) |

The **Overview** tab also includes a billing snapshot with total billed, total paid, and outstanding balance across all of the client's invoices.

## Billing and Invoices

A client's **Billing** tab brings together everything they owe in one place: invoices from all of their matters plus any invoices billed directly to the client, with a Total Billed, Total Paid, and Outstanding snapshot. You can create an invoice or add a flat fee for the client right from this tab.

For how client and matter invoices work together, see [Invoices](/billing/invoices).

## Inviting a Client to the Portal

Use **Invite to Portal** on the client's profile to send them a secure sign-in link. Once they accept, they get their own limited view where they can follow their matters, read messages, and see invoices, without access to the rest of your firm's data.

::: info
A client only ever sees what belongs to them. The portal is completely separate from the firm workspace your team uses.
:::

## Keeping Clients Notified

When you share a document with a client, the portal tells them automatically. A notification bell in the client's portal shows a badge, they receive an email, and the newly shared file is marked **New** on their Documents page until they open it. Share several files at once and the client gets a single combined notification rather than one per file, so you can share freely without flooding their inbox.

You control this from the sharing dialog: a **Notify client(s)** checkbox (on by default) lets you share quietly when a notification is not needed.

::: tip
Editing one of a client's contact details never clears the others. If you update a client's name, their phone, email, organization, and address stay intact.
:::
