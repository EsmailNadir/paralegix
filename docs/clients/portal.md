# The Client Portal

The **client portal** is the separate, limited view your clients sign into. It is not a stripped-down copy of your workspace: it is its own app, and a client only ever sees records that belong to them.

This page describes what your clients see, so you know what you are giving them access to when you send an invitation.

## Inviting a Client

Open the client's profile and use **Invite to Portal**. They receive an email with a secure link, create their account or sign in, and land in the portal.

A client can belong to more than one firm. If yours is not the only one, they pick which firm they are looking at from a switcher at the top of the portal sidebar.

## What a Client Sees

| Section | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | A summary across their matters |
| **Messages** | Their conversations with your firm |
| **My Matters** | The matters they are a client on |
| **Calendar** | Events on their matters that you marked visible to the portal |
| **Documents** | Files you have shared with them |
| **Documents to Sign** | Signing requests waiting on them |
| **Billing** | Their invoices and payment history |
| **Trust Account** | Their retainer balance and its transactions |
| **Settings** | Their notification and contact preferences for your firm |

Which sections appear depends on your firm's plan and settings. Messaging, document sharing, document signing, and trust accounting each appear only if that feature is enabled for your firm, so a client of a firm that does not use trust accounts never sees a Trust Account item.

## What a Client Cannot See

- Any matter, task, invoice, or document that is not theirs.
- Your internal notes, your team's time entries, and your firm's other clients.
- Matter events you did **not** mark visible to the client portal.
- Which other clients share a group conversation with them, if you have turned on [client name privacy](/messaging/overview#client-privacy-in-group-conversations) for that thread.

Visibility is opt-in on your side. A document appears in the portal only once you make it visible to the client, and an event only once you tick the portal-visible box.

## What a Client Can Do

- **Read and reply to messages** from your firm.
- **Download documents** you have shared, and upload documents you have requested.
- **Fulfil document requests**, working down the checklist you posted. They can preview anything they have uploaded and remove their own upload if they attached the wrong file, though they cannot remove a file the firm linked in on their behalf.
- **Sign documents** sent to them for signature.
- **View and pay invoices** online, if you have set up online payments.
- **Fund a retainer by ACH or card**, when you send them a deposit link. Requesting an electronic trust deposit is something you initiate from your side (a Checkout link you copy and share); the portal itself only shows the client their trust balance and ledger, not a self-serve "add funds" button.

## Notifications

Clients are told when something happens without you having to email them: a bell badge in the portal plus an email when you share documents, post a document request, send an invoice, or record a payment. Shares made in one sitting collapse into a single email rather than one per file.

You control the client-facing side of this from your firm's billing and sharing settings, and from the **Notify client(s)** checkbox when you share a file.

## Their Own Account

Portal users have the same personal **Account** page your team does, reached from the bottom of the portal sidebar. It holds their profile photo, display name, and personal data controls. Preferences that belong to your firm, such as how they want to be contacted, stay on the portal's **Settings** page.
