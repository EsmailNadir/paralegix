# Billing

The **Billing** section is where you log your work, turn it into invoices, and track what you have been paid. Open **Billing** from the left sidebar to reach the billing hub. This page is the map of the whole section; each part has its own page with the details.

## The Billing Hub

The hub gives you a running picture of your firm's money at a glance.

Four tiles across the top summarize where things stand:

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting payment** | The total of open invoices you have sent |
| **Unbilled work** | Time and expenses logged but not yet invoiced |
| **Overdue** | Invoices that are past their due date |
| **Collected** | Payments you have received |

These totals cover your firm's entire book of business, every matter and client, not just the matters you personally work on, so the "awaiting payment" and "overdue" figures reflect the whole firm's accounts receivable.

Below the tiles, the **Needs attention** list surfaces what to act on next: overdue invoices you can record a payment against, and unbilled work that is ready to turn into an invoice with a single **Bill now** click.

## The Billing Tabs

The tabs at the bottom of the hub each open a different part of billing:

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Upcoming payments** | Unpaid invoices grouped by when they are due. See [Payments and Plans](/billing/payments) |
| **Invoices** | Every invoice, in any status. See [Invoices](/billing/invoices) |
| **Time entries** | All logged time and unbilled work. See [Time and Expenses](/billing/time-and-expenses) |
| **Recurring plans** | Retainers that bill automatically. See [Payments and Plans](/billing/payments) |
| **Reports** | A financial roll-up by matter or client. See [Reports](/billing/reports) |
| **Rates & setup** | Hourly rates and charge codes. See [Time and Expenses](/billing/time-and-expenses) |

## From Work to Payment

The billing flow moves in a simple line, and each step has its own page:

1. **Log your work** as time entries, flat fees, and expenses. See [Time and Expenses](/billing/time-and-expenses).
2. **Generate an invoice** from that unbilled work, for one matter or many at once. See [Generating Invoices](/billing/generating-invoices).
3. **Send** the invoice and **record payments**, including payment plans and online payments. See [Payments and Plans](/billing/payments).
4. **Track** everything with [Reports](/billing/reports).

::: tip
The **Log time** and **New** buttons at the top of the hub are shortcuts you can use from anywhere in billing to record work, add an expense or flat fee, start a timer, or run bulk invoicing.
:::

::: info
Money is always shown to the cent, and every payment you record updates the matter, the client's balance, and your firm totals automatically.
:::
