# Billing Reports

The **Reports** tab in the [Billing](/billing/overview) hub gives you a financial roll-up of your practice, so you can see what has been billed, what is outstanding, and where the money is.

## Grouping

Use the **Group by** toggle to view the roll-up **by matter** or **by client**:

- **By matter** shows each matter with its total billed, outstanding balance, hours tracked, invoice count, and next payment due.
- **By client** rolls the same numbers up to the client level, across all of their matters.

You can search either view by name. **By matter** also has **Status** and **Practice Area** filters to narrow the list further; **By client** does not, since a client can span several practice areas.

## Reading a Row

Each row summarizes one matter (or client) at a glance:

- The **total billed** and the **outstanding** balance still owed.
- Counts of **clients**, **time entries**, **hours**, and **invoices**.
- The current invoices, with due dates and any that are **overdue** flagged.

Select a row to drill in. **By matter** opens that matter's own invoices list. **By client** opens the client's Billing tab, or, if the row represents billing that cannot be pinned to one client, a breakdown of which matters it came from.

::: info
The two views can leave different invoices out of their totals, and each tells you when it does. **By matter** excludes invoices billed directly to a client with no matter; **By client** excludes invoices on a matter with several clients where no single client is named. A banner appears above the table whenever a view is excluding something, telling you how much and pointing you to the other grouping to see it.
:::

::: info
The figures here come straight from your invoices and payments, so the report always matches what you see on each [invoice](/billing/invoices). Nothing to run or refresh.
:::
