# Generating Invoices

Once you have logged work, you turn it into an invoice. Paralegix gives you three ways to do this: bill one matter at a time, split a matter's bill across clients, or generate many invoices at once. For what an invoice is and how it belongs to a matter or client, see [Invoices](/billing/invoices).

## Generating an Invoice for a Matter

Open a matter, go to its **Billing** tab, and click **Generate Invoice**. This opens the invoice composer.

The composer has three parts:

- **Invoice details** on the left: who to bill (**Bill To**), an invoice name and number, the invoice and due dates, a billing email, and notes.
- **Remit To** is your firm's payment address, which appears on the invoice.
- **Invoice Summary** on the right lists the unbilled entries being included, the total hours, the subtotal, and the balance due.

Review the selected work, fill in the details, then click **Generate Invoice**. The entries you included are marked as billed so they are never invoiced twice.

::: tip
The quickest path is from the billing hub: find a piece of unbilled work in the **Needs attention** list and click **Bill now**. That drops you straight into the composer with the work already selected.
:::

## Splitting an Invoice Across Clients

When a matter has more than one client, the composer offers a **Split Across Clients** mode next to **Single Client**. Use it to divide one matter's charges among several clients, either evenly or by a share you set, so each client gets their own invoice for their portion.

## Bulk Invoicing

To bill many matters or clients in one go, use the **Bulk Invoice Generator** (the **New** menu in the billing hub).

It walks through three steps:

1. **Select** the matters and clients with unbilled work you want to invoice. The running total shows how much you are about to bill.
2. **Configure** the shared invoice settings, such as dates.
3. **Generate** all the invoices at once.

This is the fastest way to run end-of-month billing across all your matters.

::: info
However you create an invoice, it lands in the [Invoices](/billing/invoices) list, ready to send. The next step is [Payments and Plans](/billing/payments).
:::
