## Get answers fast with AI {#export-for-ai}

Need to find something quickly? Pick the topics you care about below, download them
as a single text file, and drop it into your favorite AI assistant like ChatGPT or
Claude. Then just ask your question in plain English and get an answer in seconds,
grounded in this documentation.

Only want billing, matters, and tasks? Check those three, leave the rest, and your
file stays short and focused. Want everything? Leave it all selected.
