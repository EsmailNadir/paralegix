# Calendar

The **Calendar** is every dated thing in your firm on one grid: court dates, hearings, depositions, meetings, filing deadlines, and task due dates. Open **Calendar** from the left sidebar.

## Views

Switch between three views:

- **Month** for the shape of the month ahead.
- **Week**, the default on a desktop, for the working week hour by hour.
- **Day** for a single day in detail.

Move through time with the arrows, and **Today** jumps back to the current date. Your chosen view is remembered, so the calendar opens the way you left it.

## What Appears on It

The calendar draws from everything with a date on it:

- **Matter events** such as court dates, hearings, depositions, and filing deadlines, including everything created by a [deadline set](/settings/deadline-sets).
- **Client events** such as meetings and calls.
- **Tasks** with a due date.
- **Invoice and billing dates**, if your role can view invoices: a payment due date, the next scheduled payment on a plan, and paid dates.
- **Events imported from your connected Google or Outlook calendar**, if you have sync turned on.

Tasks are colored by their state so the grid is readable at a glance: completed tasks green, urgent red, in progress amber, blocked purple, and everything else blue.

## The Day Panel

Click any day, or any block in the week and day views, to open the side panel for that day. It lists everything scheduled, and from there you can:

- Open an event or task to see its full detail and the matter it belongs to.
- Mark a task **complete**, or reopen it, without leaving the calendar.
- Mark an event **completed**, or back to upcoming. To cancel an event instead, do that from the matter's own **Events** tab.
- Mark an invoice payment as **paid**, right from its date.
- Edit or delete an item.

## Adding Events and Tasks

Use **New Event** or **New Task** in the day panel. Clicking an empty slot in the week or day view pre-fills the date and time, so scheduling something for Thursday at 2pm is two clicks.

An event can be tied to a matter or a client, given a location and a court, assigned to team members, and marked **visible to the client portal** if the client should see it. Switch on **All day** on an event or task that has no specific time, such as a filing deadline. It shows in its own row at the top of the week and day views instead of the timed grid. Picking a court fills in the location for you; if you type a different address, Paralegix offers to save it back to that court so the next event there is prefilled too.

## Reminders

Matter events get reminder emails automatically: 7 days before, 1 day before, on the day, and afterwards for up to a week if the event was never marked complete or cancelled. Recipients are the people assigned to the event, and each person sees the time in their own timezone.

Nothing to configure. See [Reminders and Alerts](/automation/reminders-and-alerts).

## Syncing With Google and Outlook

Connect your Google or Microsoft account under **Settings → General** and your Paralegix dates stay in step with the calendar you already live in. Sync is **per person**, not per firm, so each team member connects their own account and picks their own direction: send out only, import only, two-way, or off.

Once connected, sync is continuous rather than something you press. The full behavior, including how deletions reconcile and why edits never bounce between the two systems, is covered in [Documents and Calendar](/automation/documents-and-calendar#calendar-sync).

## What Clients See

Clients have their own calendar in the portal, showing only the events on their matters that you marked visible to the client portal. Nothing else on your firm's calendar reaches them.
