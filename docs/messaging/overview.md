# Messages

Messages is the secure channel your firm uses to talk with clients inside Paralegix. Your team writes from the firm portal; each client reads and replies from their own client portal. Nothing is sent by regular email, so the whole conversation stays in one place and tied to your firm.

Open **Messages** from the left sidebar to see all of your conversations.

## Direct Conversations

A direct conversation is a private 1:1 thread between your firm and a single client. There is exactly one direct thread per client, and every member of your team with messaging access shares it (so a client always reaches "the firm", not one specific person). Each message shows who on your team sent it.

To start one, click **New Message** and pick a single client. The thread is created the moment you send the first message. (The same **New Message** button also starts a group conversation, see below, if you pick more than one participant.)

Unread messages are counted for you: your team sees a badge for client replies it has not read yet, and clients see a badge for new messages from your firm. When the other side opens the thread, a message is marked as read.

## Attaching Files

You can attach up to five files to a message. Two ways:

- **Upload from your device.** Pick files from your computer.
- **Attach from your files.** Choose a document that already lives in the firm's file manager. Paralegix copies it into the conversation so the client can open it, even though they cannot see the original file. You can only attach a file you already have access to.

## Editing and Deleting Messages

You can edit or delete your own messages for up to 30 minutes after sending. Edited messages show an "edited" marker. Deleting leaves a "message was deleted" placeholder in the thread rather than removing it entirely, because the conversation is kept as a record. You can only change your own messages, never someone else's.

## Searching Messages

Use the search box on the Messages page to find text inside your conversations. It looks through the message content itself (not just names), shows a short preview of each match, and clicking a result opens that conversation. Clients can search their own conversations the same way from their portal.

## Archiving Conversations

To tidy your inbox, you can archive a conversation. Archived threads move out of the active list into an **Archived** view and stop counting toward your unread badge. Archiving is for your firm only; it does not affect what the client sees. If a new message arrives on an archived thread, it comes back to the active list automatically.

## Group Conversations

A group conversation is a shared thread with more than one participant. Use it when several people need to see the same messages, for example a client plus a co-counsel on your team, or two clients working on the same matter. A group can also be your team only, with no client, for an internal thread.

To create one, click **New Message**, give it an optional name, choose the clients and any team members to include, and create it. You are added automatically.

- **Participants** in the conversation header shows everyone in the thread and lets your team add or remove people. Removing someone stops them seeing new messages while keeping the thread history intact.
- Adding or removing a person on a 1:1 direct thread turns it into a group, keeping the same messages.
- A group can include one client or several. If you add more than one client and have not linked the group to a matter, Paralegix asks you to confirm first, since everyone in the group can see each other.
- When you add a client to a group that already has history, choose whether they can **see previous messages**. Turn it on and they read the full conversation from the start; leave it off and they only see messages sent from that point on.
- Adding or removing someone posts a short system note in the thread itself (for example, "X was added to the conversation"), so everyone can see when the participant list changed without having to check Participants.

## Linking a Group to Matters

A group can be tagged with one or more matters so it is easy to find and file. Open a group, open its settings from the header gear, and use **Matter scope** to add or remove matters. Tagging is organizational: it does not change who is in the group, and you can scope a group to several matters at once.

## Messaging from a Matter

Every matter has a **Messages** tab. From there you can:

- Message any client on the matter individually, without involving the others.
- Open any group conversation that is scoped to the matter.
- Start a new group that is already linked to the matter.

If a matter has just one client, the tab opens straight into that client's conversation. Only clients who have a client-portal account can be messaged.

## Messaging from a Client's Page

Every client also has a **Messages** tab on their detail page. It gathers every conversation with that client in one place: their direct thread and any group they are part of. A client with a single thread opens straight into it, and you can start a new group with that client from here too.

## Messaging Clients as the Firm

By default, each message you send shows your own name to the client. If you would rather clients see your firm's name and logo as the sender, turn on **Message clients as the firm**. You can set it from the conversation settings gear (which shows a live preview of how your messages will look) or from **Settings → General**. It applies to all of your conversations, and your own team always sees that the message was really from you.

## Client Privacy in Group Conversations

By default, when two or more clients share a group, they can see each other's names. If you would rather keep clients from seeing who else is a client on a shared thread, you can turn that off for that conversation.

Open the group, open its settings from the header gear, and turn on **Hide client names from other clients**. With it on, each client sees the other clients in that group as a generic "Client" instead of their real name or photo. Your own team always sees everyone's real names. This is set per conversation, so you can hide names in one group and not another. It does not affect direct 1:1 conversations, which only ever involve one client.

## Who Can Use Messaging

Sending and viewing messages is controlled by your firm's [roles and permissions](/settings/roles): team members need the messaging permissions granted to their role. Managing a group's participants, matter scope, and privacy is limited to your team; clients can take part in a conversation but cannot change who is in it.
