# Roles and Permissions

A **role** decides what a team member can see and do in Paralegix. Every member has one role, and each role is a set of **permissions**. You manage roles under **Settings > Roles**.

## Built-in Roles

Paralegix comes with a set of ready-to-use roles, from most to least access:

| Role | Meant for |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | The person who owns the firm. Full access, including billing and firm deletion |
| **Admin** | Firm administrators who manage almost everything |
| **Attorney** | Attorneys working matters, time, and billing |
| **Paralegal** | Paralegals supporting matters and tasks |
| **Staff** | Support staff with limited access |

Each role shows how many permissions it grants. Built-in roles are marked **Built-in** and cover most firms without any changes.

## Creating a Custom Role

If the built-in roles do not fit, click **New Role** to build your own:

1. Give the role a **name**.
2. Turn individual **permissions** on or off. They are grouped by area (Firm, Members, Matters, Billing, and so on), and each one has a short description.
3. Save the role. It then appears in the list and can be assigned to members.

Use **Select All** or **Clear** to start from everything or nothing, then adjust. For example, a "Billing Clerk" role might have the billing and invoice permissions but nothing under Matters.

## Assigning a Role

You choose a member's role when you [invite them](/settings/members), and you can change it any time from the **Members** tab. Changing a role takes effect the next time that person loads the app.

::: info
Permissions are granular, so you can grant exactly what a person needs and nothing more. A member only ever sees the parts of the app their role allows, which keeps sensitive areas like billing and firm settings restricted.
:::

::: tip
Give each person the least access they need to do their job. It is easy to add permissions later if they turn out to need more.
:::
