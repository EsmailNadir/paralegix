# Firm Settings

The **Settings** area (labeled **Firm Configuration** at the top of the page) is where you manage your whole law firm: your team, roles, subscription, payments, and firm-wide preferences. Open **Settings** from the bottom of the left sidebar.

::: info
Settings is limited to roles that have firm configuration access, so not every team member will see it. See [Roles and Permissions](/settings/roles).
:::

## The Settings Tabs

Settings is organized into tabs across the top:

| Tab | What you manage |
|---|---|
| **General** | Firm contact information (used on invoices) and calendar connections (Google and Outlook) |
| **Members** | Your team members and invitations. See [Team Members](/settings/members) |
| **Subscriptions** | Your Paralegix plan and billing |
| **Payments** | Connecting a payment provider so clients can pay invoices online |
| **Roles** | Built-in and custom roles with granular permissions. See [Roles and Permissions](/settings/roles) |
| **Matter Config** | Practice areas, categories, courts, charge codes, custom fields, stages, and Document Request Presets that matters and billing draw from, plus **Lead Stages** and **Intake Forms** for the [leads pipeline](/leads/overview) |
| **Customization** | Your firm logo and branding |
| **Notifications** | Your own daily digest schedule and which in-app notifications you receive. This one is personal to you, not firm-wide |

## General

The **General** tab covers two things:

- **Law Firm Information** is your firm's name and contact details. This is what appears on invoices you send, so keep it current.
- **Calendar Connections** let you link Google or Microsoft so matter events and tasks sync to your calendar.

## Your Own Account

Firm Settings covers the firm. Your own profile lives separately, on the **Account** page, reached from your name at the bottom of the left sidebar. It holds:

- Your **profile photo**, which is also what is stamped on documents you sign.
- Your **display name**, which is what teammates and clients see on your messages and notes.
- Your **personal data controls**, where you can export a copy of your data or request deletion.

These settings follow you everywhere in Paralegix rather than applying to one firm, which is why they are not under Firm Settings. Firm-scoped preferences such as notifications and calendar connections stay on the settings pages above.
