# Team Members

Your team members are the people at your firm who log in and do the work. You manage them under **Settings > Members**, on the **Team Members** tab.

The list shows everyone on your team with their name and email. From here you can search for a member, change what they can do, or remove them. The **Clients** tab next to it lists your client-portal users, which are covered on the [Clients](/clients/overview) page.

## Inviting a Member

To add someone to your firm, click **Invite Member**, then:

1. Enter the person's **email address**.
2. Pick a **role** for them (this controls what they can access).
3. Click **Send Invite**.

The person receives an invitation email with a secure link. When they accept, they create their account (or sign in) and join your firm with the role you chose.

::: tip
Pick the role that matches what the person should be able to do. You can change it later, and you can build your own roles under [Roles and Permissions](/settings/roles) if the built-in ones do not fit.
:::

::: info
Invitations are tied to the email you enter. If someone should join as a client instead of a team member, invite them from the [Clients](/clients/overview) area, not here.
:::

## Managing Members

For each member in the list you can:

- **Change their role** to adjust what they can access.
- **Remove** them from the firm when they no longer need access. Removing a member revokes their access but keeps the firm's records intact.

A member who hasn't accepted their invite yet shows as pending, with **Resend** to send the invite email again and **Cancel** to withdraw it.

::: info
Your plan may limit how many team members you can have. If you hit the limit while inviting, you will be prompted to upgrade your [subscription](/settings/overview).
:::
