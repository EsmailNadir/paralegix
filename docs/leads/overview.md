# Leads

A **lead** is a prospective client, someone who has not yet signed on, tracked from first contact through to a decision. Leads are separate from your client list: they do not count against your plan's client limit and do not show billing or matter history, because there is not any yet.

Open **Clients** from the left sidebar, then switch to the **Leads** tab to get started.

## The Leads Tab

The Leads tab lists every lead in your pipeline, with each lead's current stage. Search by name, email, or organization. To change a lead's stage, open the lead.

## Adding a Lead

Click **New Lead** and fill in what you know: name, email, phone, organization, referral source, and any notes. Only the name is required. A new lead starts in the first stage of your pipeline.

Leads usually arrive one of two ways: entered by hand, or captured automatically through a [public intake form](#public-intake-forms).

## The Lead Detail Page

Click a lead to open its page. It shows their contact details, current stage, stage date, notes, and the status of any intake form you have sent them.

From here you can:

- **Change stage** with the quick stage dropdown in the header, without opening the full edit screen.
- Click **Edit Lead** to update any field, including the **stage date**, a date you set yourself to track something about the lead's current stage, such as a scheduled consultation or a follow-up you promised.
- **Send an intake form** to collect information directly from the lead.
- **Convert to Client** once they are ready to come on board.

## Your Pipeline (Stages)

Every firm gets a default pipeline when the firm is created: **New → Contacted → Consultation Scheduled → Retained**, with **Unresponsive** and **Not a Fit** as dead-end branches off Contacted and Consultation Scheduled. You can add, rename, remove, and reorder stages, and mark a stage as a closing stage (won or lost), from **Settings → Matter Config → Lead Stages**.

Like matter stages, a firm can run one shared pipeline or a different pipeline per practice area.

## Public Intake Forms

An intake form is a public web form you can share with prospective clients so they can tell you about their matter before you ever pick up the phone.

Set these up under **Settings → Intake Forms**. Give the form a name, optionally tie it to a practice area, and build the field list: text, text area, email, phone, and dropdown fields, each markable as required. You can also protect a form with an access code so it is not wide open to anyone with the link.

Every form gets its own public link. Share it on your website, in an email signature, or directly with a specific lead:

- **Posted publicly**: anyone who submits it either creates a new lead or, if they use an email address already in your pipeline, updates that existing lead. New leads land in the first open stage of your pipeline.
- **Sent to a specific lead**: use **Send intake form** from the lead's detail page. Paralegix emails them a link. Their answers fill in whatever is still blank on their lead record; anything you already entered is left alone.

Whenever a form is submitted, everyone at your firm who can see leads gets a notification, so a submission is never missed.

## Converting a Lead

When a lead is ready to come on board, open their detail page and click **Convert to Client**. You have two options:

- **Create a client and a matter** (the default): Paralegix creates the client record and a new matter for them in one step, ready for you to fill in the rest.
- **Client only**: check the box to create just the client record, with no matter yet. Use this when you have signed someone up but the actual matter details are not settled.

Converting is one-way. Once converted, the lead's page shows a link to the client (and matter, if one was created) it became, and the lead itself moves to your pipeline's closing stage.

::: info
Converting to a client and a matter counts against your plan's matter limit, the same as creating a matter any other way. Converting to a client only does not, since no matter is created.
:::

## Who Can Use It

Leads have their own permissions, separate from clients and matters, so you can give someone intake responsibility without giving them access to your client list:

- **View leads** to see the pipeline and lead details.
- **Manage leads** to create, edit, and delete leads, and to send and configure intake forms.
- **Convert leads** to turn a lead into a client or matter.
- **Manage lead stages** to edit the pipeline itself.

See [Roles and Permissions](/settings/roles) to assign these.
